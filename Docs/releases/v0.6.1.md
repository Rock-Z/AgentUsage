# AgentUsage 0.6.1

AgentUsage shows whether you are using limits faster or slower than their windows allow.

- Marks pace on every usage bar: a tick at the share of the limit's window still to run. Remaining usage that reaches past it is under pace; falling short of it is over.
- Keeps every accent readable on the popover. Light mode deepens pale accents, including a light macOS accent, so bars and switches stay clear on any wallpaper.
- Adds space between one usage bar and the next limit's label.
